Elbow method

k was chosen as the point farthest from the straight line joining the first and last inertia values (the "elbow"): k = {{ r.elbow.chosen_k }}. Inertia always decreases as k grows; silhouette is shown as a second opinion. The curve bends only gently, so the data has no sharply separated groups - treat the clusters as patient segments, not natural classes.
